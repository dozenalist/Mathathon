VERDICT

Almost all of the arithmetic content of our framework is already explicit in
Fauzan, including several connections that we previously emphasized as an
explanatory gain. It is appropriate to retain these as attributed exposition.
They should not be presented as discoveries of a missing connection or as new
arithmetic lemmas. Our added content in this family is mainly the Volkenborn
interpretation and the link between the same Laurent coefficients in real
Euler--Maclaurin asymptotics and p-adic convergence. Replacing the tail
primitives by periodic Bernoulli notation is an exact classical identification,
but algebraically a very light change to formulas already printed by Fauzan.

1. THE PULLBACK AND THE REASON FOR LOCALIZATION: EXPLICIT IN ORIGINAL

Original section 3 opening, p. 5, explicitly explains that t=-x^2 changes square
poles to integer poles, allowing Bernoulli multiplication by residue class.
It announces extension to convergent p-adic expansions and distribution.
Equation (3.1), p. 6, is exactly mu_X(R)=tau_X(x^5 R(-x^2)). The polynomial
functional tau(P)=L(P''')/24 with L(x^k)=B_k is on p. 5. Reflection and difference
laws are equations (3.2)-(3.3), p. 6. None of these are new identifications.

2. TATE COMPLETION, NEAR/FAR POLES: EXPLICIT IN ORIGINAL

Section 3.1, pp. 6-7, defines the same continuous extension on Q_p<z>, proves
the valuation bound on its moments, and constructs the direct sum with near
principal parts. Lemma 3.1, equation (3.4), includes precisely the conditions
on unit-separated poles, harmonic indices, and leading degree.
Equation (3.5), p. 7, is the convergent far-pole Laurent series reproduced in
our equation labelled farpole, after the harmless substitution s=-a.
The analytic/principal-part decomposition and its uniqueness are already
explained there. Our exposition changes terminology/order, not the construction.

ADDED CLASSICAL INTERPRETATION: The original does not name Volkenborn integration.
Writing L(P)=I_p(P), hence tau(P)=I_p(P''')/24, identifies the same Bernoulli
functional with a standard p-adic integral. This is a legitimate additional
interpretation, not a new distribution theorem. The original derives that
theorem directly from Bernoulli multiplication.

ADDED CROSS-REALIZATION OBSERVATION: The explicit statement that the far-pole
coefficients are also the real asymptotic coefficients of -zeta(5,a) does not
appear in the original. It is a meaningful bridge between its real Hermite
calculation and p-adic completion, using classical Euler--Maclaurin. It must
retain our qualification that convergence/asymptotics in different fields do
not identify numerical values across those fields.

3. THE PARAMETER Y=p^5 X+C_p IS FORCED AT 1/x: EXPLICIT IN ORIGINAL

Section 3.2, p. 7, says explicitly that the new parameter is chosen so that
distribution holds at 1/x, after which translation and reflection determine
all integer poles. Equation (3.6) defines precisely our C_p and Y. Its
C_p in p^5 Z_p estimate is given immediately after (3.6).
Lemma 3.2, equation (3.7), pp. 7-8, is the same distribution identity.
Its proof evaluates the right side at 1/x as p^-5(-Y+C_p)=-X, and then uses
reflection and telescoping translation exactly as our note does.

Thus the claim that the new parameter is forced, and the explanation of why
the fifth power occurs, are already present in substance. Our 'four powers
from the functional and one from the pole' wording is a gloss on the displayed
calculation, not a missing conceptual result supplied by us.

4. THREE ADAPTED BASES AND CRT: EXPLICIT IN ORIGINAL

Section 3.3, pp. 8-9, expressly uses the binomial basis for integer-valued
polynomials and then the weighted f_i, with matrix (3.11) and estimate (3.12).
Section 4 opening, p. 9, explicitly says two different basis choices are needed:
vanishing distributed among classes for inner primes, and separation of residue
terms from a polynomial correction of bounded rank for outer primes.

Inner: section 4.1, p. 10, equation (4.5), gives E_{a,i}. The paragraph after
(4.5) explicitly invokes the Chinese remainder decomposition by powers of
t+c^2 and proves the basis is Z_p-unimodular. It explains allocation of L_a
so no class gives a smaller bound than the assigned row weights. Equations
(4.6)-(4.8), pp. 10-11, give exactly the weights and determinant sum.
Proposition 4.1's proof ends with the row-plus-column valuation argument and
unimodular congruence. Our common-pairing language is coordinate-free restatement.

Outer: section 4.2, pp. 11-12, first removes nonintegral polynomial moments and
proves G_K=A+p^-1 L with the rank bound (4.10). It then gives the exact-class
factors Q_a,P_a,E_a and rows P_a q_{a,i} in (4.11), with a unimodularity proof
via monic degrees and unit resultants. Calling this the CRT algebra of T_K is
an accurate packaging, but no newly discovered basis or mechanism.

The distinction between the auxiliary inner jet algebra and the actual outer
pole algebra is useful explicit bookkeeping in our note. It prevents a false
identification; it does not replace any step in the source.

5. WEIGHT-PLUS-RANK LEMMA: SAME STATEMENT AND PROOF

Original Lemma 4.2, equation (4.13), p. 12, is exactly
v det(A+p^-1 L) >= 2 sum w_i - min(r,z), with nonpositive half-integer weights
and z zero weights. The original proof expands complementary minors, sorts
the weights, writes the same maximal loss, and observes increments one at zero
weights and nonpositive afterward. Our paragraph is effectively an abridged
restatement of that proof, including closely matching mathematical wording.
It needs a direct attribution to Lemma 4.2, not only the global Scope citation.

The need to retain polynomial corrections is not our warning alone: it is
explicitly highlighted already in the introduction, pp. 2-3, and implemented
in (4.10). Our assertion that residue algebra does not determine the whole
functional is explanatory confirmation of a concern the author anticipated.

6. LOCAL COUNTS -> PROFILES -> PRIME INTEGRALS: EXPLICIT IN ORIGINAL

Section 5.1, p. 14, begins by saying discrete pole counts become floor functions
and the weighted allocation has a limiting form. Equations (5.4)-(5.7) give
Gamma, N, R=-Gamma-N and the same asymptotic rescalings. The proof following
(5.7) explicitly writes ell_A(a)=ell(A/p,a/p).
Section 5.2, p. 15, separates the outer residue contribution and rank correction
in (5.8)-(5.9) and writes the same -gamma_out=K(R0-d)+O(1). It explicitly says
the prime number theorem converts the local bounds into integrals, proved in
Proposition 5.2, equation (5.11).

Calling these functions accounting profiles rather than new moment functionals
is a helpful summary, but their relationship is fully spelled out in the source.

7. PERIODIC BERNOULLI PRIMITIVES: EXACT REWRITING OF ORIGINAL FORMULAS

Section 5.3, p. 16, gives F in (5.12), then explicitly defines P and C as
continuous periodic functions, gives their derivatives P'=F+lambda and
C'=P-Pbar, and carries out the same two integrations by parts in (5.15).
The original's G0(v)=v(1-v)(2v-1)/6 equals -B_3(v)/3, while
v(1-v)=1/6-B_2(v) and v-1/2=B_1(v). Substituting these three polynomial
identities produces every formula labelled tail1-tail3 in our note.

The phrase 'periodic Bernoulli primitives of the lattice-counting error' is an
added classical interpretation. The primitive functions, their derivatives,
their normalization constants, their bounds, and their use are all Fauzan's.
This is not an independent Euler--Maclaurin evaluation or a replacement proof.

8. OVERALL PROOF NARRATIVE: SOURCE IS ALREADY EXPLANATORY

Section 1.1, pp. 2-3, already presents the argument as one affine Hankel matrix,
positive moments at zeta(5), a squared Vandermonde integral/logarithmic energy
bound, coefficientwise local estimates on the full functional, three prime
ranges/bases, and normalization costs smaller than real decay. It then explains
Appendix C as control of the slope matrix relative to the positive matrix.
Section 2 opening, p. 3, even contrasts the residue source of degree with the
integral source of positivity in adjacent sentences.

Consequently our outline does reorganize the paper, but should not imply that
Fauzan fails to connect these layers or leaves all the named functions unrelated.
An honest description is 'a classical interpretation and reorganized exposition
of Fauzan's existing architecture, supplemented by selected corollaries'.

ATTRIBUTION RECOMMENDATIONS

* State at the start that the exact pullback, positive integral, local analytic
  completion, parameter distribution, basis mechanisms, rank lemma and profile
  calculations are Fauzan's, with specific source equation references.
* Label the rank lemma 'Fauzan, Lemma 4.2' wherever stated; its proof is also his.
* Label periodic formulas 'Fauzan's section 5.3 in Bernoulli notation'.
* Reserve 'additional interpretation' for Volkenborn realization, cross-field
  Laurent-series correspondence, and the explicitly separated algebra viewpoint.
* Do not market our proposed reordering as discovering the local-to-global
  architecture; the author's introduction already supplies it.
