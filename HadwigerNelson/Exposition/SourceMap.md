## Suggested educational figure

Draw a segment $K$, its radius-one capsule, the two endpoint disks, and a point $z\in\Delta(K)$. Show the unit circle centred at $z$ meeting the segment. For the exact region, use distance to the full segment for the minimum and distance to the farther endpoint for the maximum. Endpoint comparisons alone omit valid straddling points. Strictly inside one endpoint disk and strictly outside the other closed disk is a sufficient subset; a literal symmetric difference also includes inappropriate equality cases. Caption the figure “geometric contact lemma”; identify the additional analytic support-exclusion hypothesis separately before colouring the region as forbidden for two labels.
