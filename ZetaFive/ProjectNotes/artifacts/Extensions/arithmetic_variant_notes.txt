THE D_N^8 REPAIR: WHAT IT FIXES, AND WHAT IT CHANGES

This is a separate candidate construction. It keeps K=40n, N=3n,
h=37n, but replaces the numerator D_N^6 by D_N^8. The integer-
valued basis now uses (D_N/(N!)^2)^4 q_i, so the scalar becomes
S8=(K!)^(2h)4^(h-1)/[(N!)^(16h) product_{i=1}^{h-1}((2i)!)^2].
It is not legitimate to retain the old S_K or the old real bound U.

For the inner prime range set b=4 ell(alpha x,z), H=lambda+4alpha
=1+3alpha, T=floor(2Hx), rem=Hx-T/2, q=floor(2x),
nplus=x-q/2. The new limiting functions are
Gamma8(x)=integral_0^(1/2) (T-b)(T+b-ell(x,z)-7) dz
          +rem(2T-q-7)+(rem-nplus)_+,
N8(x)=2lambda x floor(x)-16lambda x floor(alpha x)-2J(lambda x),
R8(x)=-Gamma8(x)-N8(x).
The small-prime coefficient remains 8lambda/M. The numerator degree
is <=16N+4h+3=4.9K+3, still below the original 5K threshold for
the asymptotic values of K. The same inner CRT basis has
nonnegative class dimensions; the source's degree bounds have ample
room under p>200M.

The outer polynomial correction has rank at most
r8=max(0,K+6N-3p+3).
Its residue weights are
min(0,i+4delta-(ell+6)/2), i<ell-2, and zero otherwise.
For delta=1, these equal the ORIGINAL zeta(5) weights exactly.
Thus the removed-class cost and zero-weight table are restored.
For delta=0, the cost is still 9(ell-2) instead of 7(ell-2).
Writing v,u,t_p as in the paper gives
gamma8_out=
 -9(K-p)+8t_p-1-min(r8,p-1-N+u),             K<2p;
 -9(K-p)+5+16N+7t_p-min(r8,p+u),            K>=2p.

The limiting raw determinant cost is, with a=alpha and y=p/K,
C8(y)=9(1-y)-16a-7min(a,1-2y)-7u
       +min((1+6a-3y)_+,y+u),
       u=(1+a-3y)_+,                         1/3<y<1/2;
C8(y)=9(1-y)-8min(a,1-y)-8(1+a-2y)_+,        1/2<y<1.
Beyond y=1 the cost is zero. The scalar contribution in the outer
range is unchanged because p>N. Exact rational integration gives
integral C8=84097/57600,
Iout8=453803/288000=1.575704861111111...

FINITE INNER INTEGRATION

arithmetic_exact_inner.py evaluates R in rational arithmetic. All
possible breakpoints on [3,M] occur when one of the following is an
integer:
x, 2x, 2alpha x, 2lambda x, 2Hx,
(1-alpha)x, (1+alpha)x, 2(H-1)x.
The first five freeze the floor values. The middle two determine
when the jumps in ell(x,z) and ell(alpha x,z) exchange their order
as functions of z. The last one covers the zero of rem-nplus.
Between consecutive breakpoints the overlap lengths are affine in x
and every floor value is fixed, so R is affine in x. Its integral
against x^-3 is therefore rational and can be computed exactly.
The script evaluates at rational points, verifies the affine identity
at an additional point, and integrates the affine coefficients.

Validation: for the original r=3,s=5, the result on [3,20] is
322437603634266857629/7535670527041937280000, exactly the source's
equation (5.18), not merely the same decimal.

For D8 at zeta(7), the integral on [3,20] is
120667538150827615739401/708353029541942104320000.
The integral on [3,200] is an exact rational with a long numerator
and denominator, stored in arithmetic_variant_results.json; its
decimal value is 0.11039170983130402. It requires 1508 intervals.
Thus the cutoff-M=200 normalization upper coefficient is exactly
Iout8+8lambda/200+integral_3^200 R8(x)/x^3 dx,
with decimal value 1.723096570942415.

This is an exact integration of the proposed arithmetic certificate,
not a formal proof of all generalized local estimates. Those
estimates are supplied as mathematical derivations above and in
arithmetic_notes.txt.

THE ANALYTIC PRICE OF THIS REPAIR

The field becomes
V8(t)=V6(t)-2 integral_0^alpha log(t+u^2) du,
and the scalar constant becomes
C8*=C6*+4alpha lambda(1-log alpha).
The latter increment is about 0.9962991384. The original comparison
measure cannot simply be declared to satisfy the old bound for V8.
The root agent separately evaluates its potential and records
exploratory results. The arithmetic calculation by itself does not
claim success or prove that every possible D8 certificate fails.

A RIGOROUS SINGLE-POINT OBSTRUCTION FOR THE ORIGINAL MEASURE

experiments/single_point_certificate.py supplies a stronger, narrowly
scoped check than a numerical search for the maximum. Put t=3/4.
This lies to the right of all sixteen intervals in the original
measure. Let phi8(t)=2U_rho(t)-V8(t). Every admissible upper bound
M8 for phi8 must satisfy M8>=phi8(3/4). Consequently the same-
measure certificate coefficient obeys
A8,200+lambda M8-I(rho)+C8*
 >= A8,200+lambda phi8(3/4)-I(rho)+C8*.

Rational interval arithmetic proves that the right side is in
[1.305909182511730956, 1.305909182511731277], hence is >13/10.
The real part lambda phi8(3/4)-I(rho)+C8* is approximately
-0.417187388430684. This is a LOWER witness for the best possible
upper-bound coefficient obtained from this particular measure; it
is not an upper bound on the real determinant itself.

The computation uses exact rational interval endpoints rounded
outward to the grid 10^-18. Square roots use integer isqrt. Logs
use log(x)=2 sum z^(2k+1)/(2k+1), z=(x-1)/(x+1), after range
reduction to x in [1,2], with an explicit geometric remainder.
Arctangents use a half-angle reduction and the alternating-series
remainder. Pi uses Machin's identity 16atan(1/5)-4atan(1/239).
All numerical output decimals are displays of certified rational
intervals; they are not the source of the inequalities.

Thus the D8 repair fails with the original parameters, original
measure, and this arithmetic certificate, even if the maximum of
the potential is bounded perfectly. This does NOT prove failure
for a different comparison measure, better arithmetic content,
different alpha, or every construction involving D_N^8.
