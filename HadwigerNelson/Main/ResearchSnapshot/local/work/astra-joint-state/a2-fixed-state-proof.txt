A2: A THREE-CENTRE PROOF OF FIXED-STATE SUPPORT EXCLUSION
14 November 2026. Lead paper proof; independent review and Lean pending.

EXACT STATEMENT
Let c:C->Fin k be measurable, hw:WeakColoring k c. Fix x:C,
J,K subsets of Fin k, X:Fin k, and assume
  for every i, i not in J union K iff i=X.
Let A,B subsets of R with
  hA: RealState hc x A J,
  hB: RealState hc x B K,
  0 < volume A.
If b,d belong to supp(volume restricted to B), then
  |direction(b)-direction(d)| != 1.
No FourCenter, FiveCenter, interface data, positivity of B, or finite-state
encoding is added. A need not be periodic. This is exactly the original
fixed-state contract.

PROOF
Suppose |direction(b)-direction(d)|=1. Set
  y=x+direction(b), z=x+direction(d).
Thus x,y,z form an equilateral unit triangle.

BoundaryTrace:24 state_support_avoiding_trace, applied to hB at b and d,
gives one trace at y and one at z, each identically zero on every label
in K. BoundaryTrace:53 avoiding_trace_palette therefore gives
  P_y(t) is not a subset of K,
  P_z(t) is not a subset of K,
for almost every angular t.

FiveSupport:23 palette_neighbor_disjoint (general in k) gives, for almost
every angular t,
  P_y(t) disjoint P_x(t),
  P_z(t) disjoint P_x(t),
  P_y(t) disjoint P_z(t).
For the third use, choose an angle alpha with direction(alpha)=z-y;
this is possible because |z-y|=1, and apply the same theorem at y.
All three centres and angles are fixed before taking exceptional sets.

Pull these five conull statements to R by angleMk_quasiMeasurePreserving.
From hA, after one FINITE intersection over labels in J,
  t in A implies J subset P_x(t)
for almost every real t. Because volume A>0, choose a t in A satisfying
all six statements. Select i in P_y(t)\K. Disjointness from P_x(t) and
J subset P_x(t) show i not in J. Hence i=X, and X belongs to P_y(t).
The same argument gives X in P_z(t). This contradicts disjointness of
P_y(t),P_z(t). QED.

FINITE LABEL FACT USED
For any finite label universe, if the complement of J union K is {X},
J subset P, Q disjoint P, and Q not subset K, then X belongs to Q.
Proof: choose i in Q\K, use disjointness to exclude i in J, then use the
complement identity. No palette cardinality bound or disjointness J,K
is required. This is the general-label version of the forcing step in
FiveSupport.five_support_step_ban.
