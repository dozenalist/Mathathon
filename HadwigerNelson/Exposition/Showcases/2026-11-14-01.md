# First showcase of the proof explanations

14 November 2026, Pacific time. This is a showcase of new teaching material, not an announcement of a new chromatic bound.

The [proof walkthrough](../ProofWalkthrough.md) now gives one connected route through the supplied argument: recover measurable structure from an arbitrary colouring, extract palettes and interfaces, and rule out the colour cycles that topology forces. It includes a worked density-point argument to explain how an almost-everywhere condition can have exact geometric consequences.

The [unit-distance model](../Tools/UnitDistanceContact.md) lets a reader move a point around a connected segment. Try **Middle matters**. Both endpoints are more than one away, yet the circle meets the interior twice. This is why a picture based only on endpoint disks would lose valid exclusions. The precise idea is that the full set of distances is an interval.

The [cross-project briefing](../Progress/2026-11-14-first-briefing.md) explains the team's results and their scope. The [reader checks](../ReaderChecks.md) ask the reader to reconstruct the key distinctions in their own words. The [source map](../SourceMap.md) connects the exposition to exact theorem statements and preserved evidence.

The [three-centre walkthrough](../ThreeCentres.md) now turns a proof simplification into a contradiction with three finite sets, then explains exactly how traces and almost-everywhere palette facts supply those sets. The later [geometry update](../Progress/2026-11-14-geometry-update.md) explains two major paper results: curved interfaces defeat a generic endpoint extension, and two odd walks exhaust a central four-label list.

The [paired-configuration addendum](../Progress/2026-11-14-paired-configuration.md) connects those odd walks back to six interfaces in one exact paired-direction configuration. A six-row table shows how the actual palette is forced. The [two completed Claude reviews](../Reviews/Claude-FirstReview.md) improved the strict-boundary and density-measure explanations after independent checking.

The next teaching targets are the source of two-label interfaces, the geometric reason the closed 60-degree endpoint works, and the three-centre palette contradiction. Each should include a concrete configuration, its exact hypothesis, and the implication it adds to the full proof.
