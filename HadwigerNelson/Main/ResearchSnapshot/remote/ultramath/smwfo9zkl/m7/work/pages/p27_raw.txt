5.2 Exclusion, density, and smooth transport
Lemma 5.2 (Palette transfer). The following assertions hold at every fixed center x.
1. For almost every independent-angle pair (e,v) ∈ S × S,
c(x + e + v) / ∈ Px(e) ∪ Px(v). (5.2)
2. If e is an angular density-one point of Dx,i = {e′ : i ∈ Px(e′)}, then for each v nonparallel to e, the
spatial density of Ai at x + e + v is zero.
