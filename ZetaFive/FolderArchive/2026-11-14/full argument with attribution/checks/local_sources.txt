Mapping to local_arithmetic.tex:

1. Gauss valuations and weighted determinants:
   Apery/Arith/Val.lean, det_GV;
   Apery/Arith/BasisChange.lean, det_basis_change.
   Standard permutation expansion and bilinear basis change.

2. tau, pole extension and pullback:
   Fauzan section 3, equation (3.1).
   Apery/Arith/PoleFun.lean, tauX, dd;
   Apery/Arith/Pullback.lean, muX_eq_tauX.
   The full pullback proof is retained in the root appendix.

3. Integer-valued polynomial estimate:
   Fauzan equation (3.9).
   Apery/Arith/BinomBasis.lean;
   Apery/Arith/TauBound.lean, tau_bound;
   Apery/Arith/SmallPrime.lean, VG_eval_int_of_window.
   The exposition derives the binomial identities from formal generating
   functions and gives the valuation proof.

4. Coarse rational-function estimate:
   Apery/Arith/SmallPrimeTau.lean, crude_tauX.
   Direct integer-window proof with residues at +/-1,...,+/-K;
   it replaces the original analytic-ball argument.
   The factorial ratio and elementary binomial valuation bound are shown.

5. Coarse bound on F:
   Fauzan (3.11)--(3.12).
   Apery/Arith/SmallPrimeF.lean, small_prime_bound.
   Exact q_i basis, integral evaluations, determinant normalization.

6. Direct residue-class lemma including polynomial parts:
   Apery/Arith/DirectBound.lean, VG_res;
   Apery/Arith/DirectPoly.lean, VG_polyPart_eval, tauX_direct.
   The substitution x=m+pz and monic-division argument are reproduced
   mathematically, not replaced by a code citation.

7. Inner CRT basis and capped row weights:
   Fauzan section 4.1.
   Apery/Arith/ClassFrame.lean, class_frame;
   Apery/Arith/ClassCount.lean, Bse;
   Apery/Arith/InnerWeights.lean, inner_bound_cap.
   Zero count has the additional 5 from x^5; s_H(0) is doubled.

8. Water-filling allocation:
   Apery/Arith/InnerAlloc.lean, kfSel, PhiK_le_kf.
   Apery/Norm.lean, betaI, kloI, ktopI, L0I, allocI.
   The exposition proves the uniform O_400(1) version used by the
   growth section, instead of carrying the formal source's large
   explicit auxiliary constants. No change in the chosen allocation.

9. Outer basis and nonpositive weights:
   Fauzan section 4.2.
   Apery/Arith/OuterBasis.lean, rootsO;
   Apery/Arith/OuterEntryMain.lean, wO, outer_entry, outer_bound.
   The exposition writes the root multisets as polynomial products.

10. Pole congruence and two-pole cancellation:
    Apery/Arith/PoleVal.lean, GV_poleValue_congr;
    Apery/Arith/OuterEntries.lean, pair_GV;
    Apery/Arith/OuterEntryMain.lean, pole_case_a, pole_case_b,
    pole_case_zero.
    Harmonic congruence, divided differences and both zero-class cases
    are explained explicitly.

11. Bernoulli high-moment correction and rank loss:
    Apery/Arith/MomentVal.lean, VG_muMono_int;
    Apery/Arith/OuterFrame.lean, outer_frame;
    Apery/Arith/LowRank.lean, det_lowrank_bound.
    Uses the standard von Staudt--Clausen theorem, stated in text.
    Full loss r=max(0,52n+2-2p), NOT the original min(r,z).
    Block determinant proof is included.

12. Uniform conditions:
    Sufficient conservative threshold K>480000, n>=12001.
    Then p>K/400 implies p^2>3K, stronger than the source's listed
    separation and degree conditions. Only eventual bounds are needed.

Coverage:
  All new argument-specific local inequalities are proved in prose.
  Standard facts used: finite-difference interpolation, elementary
  arithmetic of valuations, Chinese remainder theorem, Legendre's
  factorial valuation formula, von Staudt--Clausen, determinant identities.
  The source-dependent choices of bases, weights and allocation are
  explicitly credited. Finite checks are supplementary and do not
  substitute for the general proofs.
