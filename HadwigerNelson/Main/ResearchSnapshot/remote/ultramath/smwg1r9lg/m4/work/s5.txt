5     Circle palettes and their cardinality
We now assume a weak measurable five-coloring and develop angular palettes at every center.
Their labels record all possible limiting circle samples. The main result of this section is that
almost every palette contains at most two labels, at every fixed center. We obtain this bound from
exclusions between circle samples and a separation property for centers whose palettes meet one
fixed pair of labels.
