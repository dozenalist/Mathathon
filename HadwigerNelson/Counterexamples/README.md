# Negative results and corrections

The research preserves failed approaches so later work can avoid relying on them.
Use the dated corrections in the
[consolidated findings](../Main/ResearchSnapshot/local/outputs/CONSOLIDATED-FINDINGS.txt)
and [progress log](../Main/ResearchSnapshot/local/outputs/PROGRESS-LOG.txt).

- The finite five-cycle shortcut assumed validity at directions not licensed by
  the analytic hypotheses. It does not remove manuscript Lemma 6.7.
- The broad compact-extension composition claim was withdrawn after the skew-shift
  test. A conditional theorem must retain its missing hypothesis.
- The Heule 553 and translated 1,048-vertex graphs have complete separating
  families of five-colourings, closing their forced-equal-pair searches.
- The count of 95 discrete survivors was corrected to 174 after a witness
  reconstruction bug. The conditional cone filter then leaves six on that grid.
- The mixed-inner/outer aligned-axis shortcut is invalid; the accepted cone
  argument uses actual signed directions and outer-strip geometry.
- The generic above-60-degree sharpness construction does not establish sharpness
  for six colours. Its independent review is
  [here](../Main/ResearchSnapshot/scale-up/ULTRA-SHARPNESS-REFEREE.txt).
- A proposed extension of the monochromatic-cone argument to the 120-degree
  endpoint was retracted: an explicit open Reuleaux region supplies the required
  open-arc strips without a unit pair. Shrinking segments also refute a universal
  positive strip-width bound. The
  [endpoint correction](../Main/ResearchSnapshot/local/outputs/MC120-ENDPOINT-CORRECTION.txt)
  preserves the strict-aperture argument and leaves stronger endpoint theorems
  with additional hypotheses open.
