SYSTEM
F is the field of real algebraic numbers; E=F(i) has the discrete additive topology.
K={u inE:|u|=1}; D is the compact character group ofE.
Action: (u.d)(z)=d(uz). Let a_z=integral d(z) dmu(d).
Assume mu is a K-invariant Borel probability. No ergodicity assumption is needed for the lemma.

LEMMA
For nonzeroz, |a_z|=1 implies mu is the point mass at the trivial character.
Consequently a wild law, which assigns measure0 to the embedded ordinary planeC, satisfies |a_z|<1 for every nonzeroz.

PROOF
The integer additive span ofK isE. For t inF choose an integern>=1 with |t|<=2n. Put a=t/(2n), b=sqrt(1-a²), u=a+ib and v=a-ib. Bothu,v lie inK and t=n(u+v). Thus the span containsF; iK=K implies it containsiF as well. For nonzeroz, span_Z(Kz)=E.

If |a_z|=1, the identity integral|psi_z-a_z|²=1-|a_z|² gives psi_z=c almost everywhere. Invariance implies psi_uz=c almost everywhere for eachu inK. Countability gives one common conull set. Take r=(1+i*sqrt3)/2 and s=conjugate(r). Then r+s=1, so c=psi_z=psi_rz*psi_sz=c². Since |c|=1, c=1. Every character on the conull set annihilatesKz, hence allE. It is therefore trivial, proving the lemma.

CORRECTIONS TO THE DEEPSEEK PACKET
H_z={d:d(z)=1} is notK-stable for nonzeroz: its annihilatorZz is not preserved by multiplication byi. The claimed action onD/H_z therefore does not descend.
A coset's intersection with the embedded plane corresponds to a union of parallel affine lines, not a single line, because the exponential is2pi-periodic. The invariance argument above nevertheless rules out the proposed wild invariant coset-supported measure.

CORRECT REMAINING CRITERION
If mu is also ergodic, weak mixing is equivalent, along any fixed sequence of finite nonempty Følner setsPhi_n inK, to
  average overu inPhi_n of |a_(uz-w)-a_z*conjugate(a_w)|² ->0
for EVERYz,w inE. No uniformity in the two frequencies is required. If either frequency is0, the centered expression is identically0.

Justification: h_z=psi_z-a_z span the mean-zero subspace densely by Stone–Weierstrass and density of continuous functions inL²(mu), without Haar orthogonality. Their matrix coefficients are exactly the centered expression. Apply the mean ergodic theorem to centered tensor products; Cauchy–Schwarz and density prove the converse.
