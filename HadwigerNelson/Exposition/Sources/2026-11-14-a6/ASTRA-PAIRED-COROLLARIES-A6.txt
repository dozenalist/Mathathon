1. AT MOST THREE DISTINCT DIRECTIONS ARE IMPOSSIBLE

This corollary uses strict MC120 as an accepted PAPER input, pending
formal proof: directions in a cone of aperture strictly less than
120 degrees cannot jointly exclude all but one label. Its strict
endpoint is preserved throughout the following argument.

By CC60, a coincident-direction block contains at most two cycle-edge
indices, and a two-element block consists of adjacent indices. Indeed,
two nonadjacent cycle edges cover four labels, and every three distinct
cycle edges include a nonadjacent pair. Either violates CC60 in the
zero-width cone containing the block.

Consequently six edges cannot occupy fewer than three directions. If
there are exactly three, every block has size two and the blocks form
a perfect matching of the edge-index C6. Its only two perfect matchings
are
  (01),(23),(45) and (12),(34),(50).
A cyclic global relabeling reduces the second to the first.

For the first matching, the three blocks exclude respectively
  U0={0,1,2}, U1={2,3,4}, U2={0,4,5}.
Any two block unions have exactly five labels. If their directed rays
had circular separation less than120 degrees, the four associated
continua would fit in that shorter cone and contradict strict MC120.
Thus every pair of the three rays has circular separation at least120.

List the three rays in circular order, with successive positive gaps
g0,g1,g2. Circular distance at least120 implies each forward gap is at
least120, since the circular distance is the minimum of that gap and
its complementary arc. Their sum is360, so all three gaps equal120.

The configuration is therefore equilateral and paired. A common rotation
or reflection puts the three blocks in the normalized order of A6; its
global-label symmetry handles the other perfect matching. A6 proves the
canonical actual subpalette, and accepted A5 excludes it. Contradiction.

CONCLUSION, CONDITIONAL ON STRICT MC120: under the common actual
hypotheses, the six directions cannot have at most three distinct values.
The argument did not close MC120's endpoint: equality was retained as
the equilateral case and then excluded by the separate A5/A6 theorem.
This does not classify or exclude all four-, five-, or six-direction
configurations.

2. ONE UNIFORM OPEN NEIGHBOURHOOD OF THE PAIRED CONFIGURATION IS EXCLUDED

Let the nominal directions be
  alpha^0=(0,0,2pi/3,2pi/3,4pi/3,4pi/3),
let tau=pi/3, and let the canonical sector boundary set be
  B={pi/6+k*tau : k=0,...,5}
on the angular circle. Let P be the canonical field with pairs
  A0={1,4}, A1={2,5}, A2={0,3},
constant on sectors S_j=((j-1/2)tau,(j+1/2)tau), with index mod3.

Claim: There is epsilon0>0, fixed entirely by A5's geometry, such that
the common actual hypotheses are impossible whenever
  d_circle(alpha_i,alpha_i^0)<epsilon0 for every i.       (N)
The perturbations are independent: the two directions in a nominal
pair need not remain equal. Thus this is genuinely an open six-direction
neighbourhood, not just a statement about three coincident pairs.

2a. Choose the finite obstruction BEFORE the colouring.

A5 proves by pure geometry that one finite unit-distance configuration
F exists which is not colourable from the canonical list field L.
Its finite odd connecting walks are obtained from open-set geometry
and connectedness, independently of any colouring or centre. Fix one
such F once and for all.

Every v in F lies strictly inside the A5 domain D. Choose its unordered
unit-sum representation
  v=e(a_v)+e(b_v).
Both angles are strictly inside canonical sectors, and
sin(b_v-a_v) is nonzero. There are only finitely many chosen angles, so
  eta=min_{v in F} min(d_circle(a_v,B),d_circle(b_v,B))>0.
Set, for example,
  epsilon0=min(eta/4,pi/24)>0.                         (EPS)
This has the requested strict bound epsilon0<min(eta/2,pi/12).
No numerical value of eta or epsilon0 is asserted.

2b. The safe angular set is preserved by all six shifts.

Define
  T={theta:d_circle(theta,B)>epsilon0}.
This is the union of the six canonical sectors shrunk by epsilon0 at
both ends. Since B+tau=B, T+tau=T; every one of the six tau-shifts of
a safe phase is safe.

For each nominal alpha_i^0, the zeros of cos(theta-alpha_i^0) belong
to B. At theta in T their angular distance from theta is greater than
epsilon0. Under (N), replacing alpha_i^0 by alpha_i cannot cross a
cosine zero. Therefore each of the six actual signs agrees exactly
with its nominal sign throughout T, with strict inequalities.

The source continuum restrictions only require these strict signs;
they do not require equality of the two vectors assigned to a block.
Hence the six OUT/IN sign rows in A6 remain valid on the shrunken
sectors for the actual independently perturbed directions.

Intersect the finitely many source hit conull sets and all six shifts
of the palette_sixty_disjoint conull set, exactly as in A6. For almost
every safe base phase, the six palettes simultaneously have singleton
members

and hits of
  ({3,4,5},{1,2,3},{0,1,5},{3,4,5},{1,2,3},{0,1,5}).
Both neighbours remain in T. Their singleton labels and disjointness

It follows that

for angular-a.e. theta in T. No field claim is needed near the removed
sector endpoints, and no palette-size bound has been used.

The tau-invariance of T is essential here: it supplies the neighbour
singletons on the SAME shrunken phase orbit. Shrinking unrelated arcs
without that invariance would not justify this six-shift inference.

2c. Local RealStates suffice for every vertex of the fixed F.

Every a_v and b_v has distance at least eta from B. A real interval
of radius epsilon0 about any chosen lift of one of these angles has
boundary margin at least eta-epsilon0>=3epsilon0. It is therefore
contained in T and in the same canonical sector as its centre.

On this interval the corresponding pair P(a_v), or P(b_v), is constant
and is contained in the actual palette almost everywhere. Angular-to-
real quasi-measure preservation gives the exact RealState hypothesis
on that interval. Its complement has line-density zero at its central
angle, because the angle is an interior point of the interval.

Nonparallelity is unchanged because F and its representation angles

It is NOT necessary that the fixed angles a_v,b_v themselves avoid

Apply States.finite_density_realization to the finite vertices x+F
and their fixed lists L. It supplies a proper L-colouring of F,
contradicting the A5 geometric obstruction. This proves the claim.
