## 1. `C132` — my error, and the correct derivation

The previous text chained `(4π/3)R³ − O(R²) ≥ Σ vol Ω(v) ≥ (|B_R| − |S_ε∩B_R|)(4π/3)(1+ε/2)³` and
concluded `1 ≥ c̄(1+ε/2)³`. **Invalid**: coverage is a *lower* bound on the sum, so it cannot be
combined with a lower bound on the terms to bound `c` from above. Corrected in place:

- **Upper bound (the missing half).** `Ω(v) ⊆ B(v,2)` by saturation (`|x−v| ≤ |x−u|` for all centres,
  and some centre is within `2` of `x`); cells have disjoint interiors; so `Σ_{v∈B_R} vol Ω(v) ≤
  vol B(0,R+2) = (4π/3)(R+2)³`.
- **Lower bound (branch).** `vol Ω(v) ≥ (4π/3)(1+ε/2)³` off `S_ε`.
- Hence `(|B_R|/R³)(1+ε/2)³ ≤ ((R+2)/R)³ + (|S_ε∩B_R|/R³)(1+ε/2)³`.
- **Density cases audited separately.** `|B_R| = O(R³)` (2-separation), so `|S_ε∩B_R| = o(R³)`; along a
  sequence realising `limsup` the last term vanishes and the right side tends to `1`, giving
  `c̄(1+ε/2)³ ≤ 1`; along a sequence realising `liminf`, `c_(1+ε/2)³ ≤ 1`. Both densities obey
  `δ ≤ 1/(1+ε/2)³`; existence of the limit is never assumed.
- The coverage lower bound is retained **as a lower bound**, explicitly marked as not used here.

This is exactly the repair the schedule asked for (`C132`, in place, marked as a second repair).

```
vol − V_dod  =  Σ_i (d_i − 1)·mean_i A_i  ≥  A_0 Σ_i (d_i − 1),     A_0 = 3V_dod/12 = 1.387573 .
```

```
≈ 0 (×3), 0.3533, …, 1.7409      ← the three zero modes are the global rotations ✓
```

```
⟹   σ₀ ≥ 2V_dod(1 + 3.3898·10⁻⁴)   ⟹   δ₀ ≥ 3.76·10⁻³   (1/56.6 of the sharp 0.213126)
```
