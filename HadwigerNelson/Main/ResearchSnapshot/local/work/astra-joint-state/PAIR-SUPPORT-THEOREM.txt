1. THE GENERAL THEOREM

Let I be finite, f : R -> I measurable, d real, and
  A_i = {t : f(t)=i},  S_i = supp(Lebesgue restricted to A_i),
  Q_ij = {t : f(t)=i and f(t+d)=j}.
Assume the following CONTACT BAN for every ordered pair (i,j):
  S_i intersects S_j  ==>  Lebesgue(Q_ij)=0.                 (CB)
Then, for every i and every a,
  a in S_i  ==>  a+d not in S_i.                           (NR)
This is pointwise separation of closed measure supports, not an AE conclusion.

Proof. Set K_ij = supp(Lebesgue restricted to Q_ij). The finite measurable
partition A_i = disjoint union_j Q_ij implies
  S_i = union_j K_ij.                                     (P)
Indeed K_ij is contained in S_i. If a belongs to none of the K_ij, choose
one open null neighborhood for each j and intersect the finitely many
neighborhoods. Its intersection with A_i is null, so a is not in S_i.
Translation continuity and preservation of Lebesgue null sets give
  a in K_ij ==> a+d in S_j.                               (T)
To check this carefully, if V is an open neighborhood of a+d with
Lebesgue(V intersect A_j)=0, then V-d is an open neighborhood of a and
Q_ij intersect (V-d) is contained in (V intersect A_j)-d, a null set.
Now suppose a in S_i and a+d in S_i. By (P), select ONE j with a in K_ij.
By (T), a+d in S_j. Thus S_i intersects S_j; (CB) makes Q_ij null, hence
K_ij empty, contradiction. QED.

The same proof works for a finite-valued measurable f on a second-countable
space with a support theory and a continuous, quasi-measure-preserving map T.
For formalization the R/Lebesgue/translation statement above is sufficient.
Finiteness is essential to the finite-union support argument. No density
lower bound, simultaneous choice from marginal supports, uniform Fourier
gap, weak mixing, or varying-centre continuity is used.

Equivalent interface for the EXISTING generic transport lemma:
  R(i,j) := Disjoint(S_i,S_j).
For every ordered pair whose supports intersect, (CB) excludes that pair
almost everywhere. A finite intersection over all i,j gives
  R(f(t),f(t+d)) for almost every t.
Apply FiveSupport.lean:55 stateSupport_shift_relation to get
  a in S_i ==> exists j, Disjoint(S_i,S_j) and a+d in S_j.
This immediately implies (NR). Thus a minimal Lean replacement need not
introduce six-tuples or re-prove a joint-support library.

4. WHAT THE SIX-ANGLE REPRESENTATION DOES AND DOES NOT ADD

For the actual periodic angular f let
  W(t)=(f(t),f(t+d),...,f(t+5d)), d=pi/3,
and K_w=supp(Lebesgue restricted to {t:W(t)=w}).
Then for each r in {0,...,5}:
  {a : a+rd in S_i} = union_{w:w_r=i} K_w.
If rho(w)=(w_1,...,w_5,w_0), exact 2pi-periodicity gives
  K_{rho(w)}=K_w+d.
If W belongs to an allowed finite word set almost everywhere, every
forbidden word has empty support. Conversely, the allowed word supports
cover R, since Lebesgue has full support. These statements follow from
the same finite-union argument, continuity and preservation of null sets.
They preserve joint correlations; no Cartesian product of marginal lists
may be substituted for the actual joint support family.

The source adjacency condition leaves exactly 100 closed six-step C5
walks among 5^6 raw words: six increments +/-1 must have total 0, since the
only multiple of 5 among -6,-4,-2,0,2,4,6 is 0. Thus there are 5*binom(6,3).
The return-exclusion proof above needs only the two-coordinate projection.
This is the useful reduction found by the experiment, rather than a reason
to formalize all 15,625 words.

6. A CORRECT PERIODIC, POSITIVE-MEASURE SUPPORT COUNTEREXAMPLE

This repairs both historic mistakes: countable dense sets are null and
have EMPTY restricted-measure support, and a 60-periodic A satisfies
A-60=A, not disjointness.

There is a Borel B subset (0,d), d=pi/3, such that both B and its complement
have positive measure in every nonempty open subinterval of (0,d).
Construction: enumerate the rational open subintervals I_n. Inductively
choose two positive-measure compact nowhere-dense sets K_n^0,K_n^1 inside
I_n, disjoint from one another and from all previously selected sets.
A finite union of compact nowhere-dense sets leaves an open subinterval
in I_n; a scaled positive-measure fat Cantor set fits there. Put
B=union_n K_n^0. Its complement contains every K_n^1. Thus each side has
positive measure in every I_n, hence in every open interval. B is F_sigma.

Write every real t uniquely as t=s+kd, 0<=s<d, k integer, and put
  f(t) = (k + 1_B(s)) mod 2, interpreted as states 0,1 in Z/5.
Then f is Borel, 6d-periodic, and f(t+d)=1-f(t) pointwise. Therefore its
six-angle words are the two alternating closed C5 walks, and adjacent
level sets at a 60-degree shift are disjoint pointwise.
Nevertheless S_0=S_1=R: every open real interval contains a subinterval
inside one d-sector, where both level sets have positive measure.
Consequently S_i intersects (S_i-d) in ALL of R, so even an AE upgrade
from level-set exclusion to support exclusion is false.

This is an exact measurable ANGULAR countermodel to 'periodicity +
adjacency + full joint six-word correlations imply no-sixty'. It is NOT
a countermodel to the source application, because it violates the
contact-ban premise: its 0/1 contacts coexist with positive 0->1 and
1->0 transitions. It is not a plane colouring.

Optional local realizability strengthening for the eight finite models:
replace the two-way construction by a finite Borel partition of (0,d)
into |Q| sets each having positive measure in every open interval, where
Q is the model's listed compatible joint-word family. For t=s+kd choose
the kth coordinate of the word assigned to s. This yields an actual
Borel 6d-periodic angular function with adjacency at every angle and
the EXACT prescribed support lists at every angle in the interior of
the six sectors. At any a in (0,d), it has the prescribed opposite
contacts at a and a+3d. D (neighboring support-list disjointness) holds
away from the finitely many sector boundaries, hence almost everywhere.
At a sector boundary the closed supports include the lists from both
sides; D fails there. Thus this realizes the old models under an AE-D
contract, not under the source's stronger POINTWISE five_support_no_sixty.
It still violates the contact bans, as the finite projection audit shows.
No claim of all-source analytic realizability follows.
