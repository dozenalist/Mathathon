TROUBLESHOOTING PRACTICE
Keep the four automated chats and one local Lean check at a time for now. Human/team jobs share the remote resources, so a supervisor limit alone cannot reserve the whole VM.
A serial queue limits simultaneous builds and makes individual timeouts/logs attributable. It does not guarantee that a difficult proof terminates.
For the next failure, retain its timestamp, operation/model, project/chat, HTTP status and safe error code, time to failure, and whether model streaming or just a shell command stalled. Avoid logging credentials or request headers.
Official error categories distinguish resource_exhausted(413/429), unavailable(502/503), deadline_exceeded(504), and asleep(409). A timeout alone is not evidence of exhausted model credits.
A lightweight VM snapshot can inspect nproc, free -h, df -h, running-process CPU/RSS and documented job/terminal listings. Compare before/after a single controlled job; do not deliberately saturate the team's VM.
Stop completed jobs only after verifying their ownership and status; do not restart/update the shared VM to clear contention while teammates work. No VM restart, resize, quota reset or toolchain change was performed.
Ask platform support to confirm the precise scope/counting/release rules for command slots and simultaneous inference calls if these limits are not exposed by the app/runtime.
