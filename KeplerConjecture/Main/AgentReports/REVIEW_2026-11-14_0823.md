semicontinuous, Fejes Tóth gives the global minimum `V_dod` and `C115` its unique minimiser, so
`η₀ := min over {d ≥ θ_max} of (vol − V_dod) > 0` — **by compactness, with no modulus.** Then
`δ₀ = 2V_dod·min(η₀, c θ_max²)`, and since `η₀` is not explicit, **the `δ₀` is not explicit either.**

**Where the entry lives.** On `{d ≥ θ_max}` the cell either degenerates (`vol = ∞`) or has **13 or more
facets** — so an explicit `η₀` is a lower bound for cells with `≥ 13` facets. **That is `C125`'s stopped
branch**, reappearing from the other side. The entry and the 13-face branch are the same obstruction.

So: `δ₀` is conditional on **two** things (the local rate and the entry), and `C142`/`C146`'s ladders are a
