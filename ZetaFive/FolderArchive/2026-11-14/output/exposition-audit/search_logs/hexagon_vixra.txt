DIRECT SOURCE MATCH
Chun Wing Lai, On Fauzan's Proof that zeta(5) is Irrational, v1, 29 September 2026, 19 pages:
https://ai.vixra.org/pdf/2609.0080v1.pdf
The parallel draft identifies this source/version on page 1, provides section/equation references at every main heading, and repeats the attribution on page 6. Its main constructions track Lai's Lemma 3.1 (p3), functional and moment/pole formulas (pp4-6), positive-definiteness proof (p7), Vandermonde factorization and leading coefficient (pp7-8), and corrected third-derivative pullback (p9). The overview likewise identifies Lai in its subtitle and gives section/proposition references. The same ideas and formulas are therefore credited dependence, not evidence of concealed borrowing. Lai himself attributes the construction and parameters to Fauzan on p1. The Stieltjes interpretation is not named in Lai; see separate Prévost/background investigation.

CLOSEST HEXAGON METHOD PARALLELS (PRIMARY PDF PASSAGES INSPECTED)
1. Shawn Krisman, Irrationality of chi2(1/q), Ti2(1/q) and chi2(2/q), 13 November 2026, 46 pages:
https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00432v1
Pages 8-9, sections 2.2-2.4 and Proposition 3.1/Theorem 3.2, present rational polynomial/pole decomposition, an affine functional, Hankel determinants, degree <= matrix size, change-of-basis scaling, and a small-integer contradiction. This is a real structural parallel to parallel-draft pp4-6 and overview pp1/3/5. Its poles are half-integers and its constants are chi2/Ti2; its nonvanishing mechanism is different. These are standard or shared determinant techniques. No distinctive prose match was observed. It does not establish that the draft borrowed from this paper, and no citation is demanded merely by these elementary similarities.

2. Sean Litsey, Irrationality measures for L(2,chi_-3) and other differences of trigamma values, PDF dated 1 November 2026, 40 pages:
https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00401v2
Pages 5-6 describe determinant methods, denominator estimates, smallness, and nonvanishing, and expressly cite Fauzan and Li Lai. The method uses Loewner matrices/divided differences and Andreief/energy estimates. Related to overview p4's determinant/energy discussion, but not the same construction or distinctive language. Hexagon landing-page title/version metadata differed from PDF wording, so cite PDF and its printed title.

3. Antti Hippeläinen, On the irrationality of the 3-adic zeta value zeta_3(5), October 2026, 50 pages:
https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00408v2
Pages 1-4 describe a rational-functional/Hankel strategy with arithmetic, size and nonvanishing estimates; p3 explicitly contrasts real positive-measure nonvanishing with its 3-adic case. Pages 6-7 develop harmonic-sum/pole functional expansions; p11 has Gram change-of-basis scaling. This is a related adaptation for a different constant and field, not evidence of copied draft prose. Public landing-page metadata confusingly showed v1 while the PDF identifies v2.

4. Shawn Krisman, One of zeta(7), zeta(9), ..., zeta(21) is irrational, PDF dated 11 November 2026, 34 pages:
https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00516v2
Pages 1-4 use a hypergeometric derivative/saddle-point construction and discuss Fauzan as related work. The source is relevant to the research topic, but is less similar than the determinant papers and contains no observed distinctive match to the audited exposition.
