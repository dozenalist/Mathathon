2.3. Odd zeta values. Odd zeta values ζ(3),ζ(5),ζ(7),... are much more difficult. We recall the
main results.
• Ap´ery [1] proved in 1978 that ζ(3) is irrational, using an explicit sequence of rational
approximations with exceptionally small denominators.
• Rivoal [11] proved in 2000 that infinitely many odd zeta values are irrational.
• Zudilin [13] proved in 2001 that at least one of ζ(5),ζ(7),ζ(9),ζ(11) is irrational.
• Fischler, Sprang, and Zudilin [8] improved the lower bound on the number of irrational odd
zeta values.
• Fischler [7] proved in 2026 a lower bound on the dimension of the Q-vector space spanned
by odd zeta values, using Siegel’s lemma.
• Brown and Zudilin [4] constructed the best known rational approximations to ζ(5), with
exponent 0.86.
None of these methods proves the irrationality of any specific odd zeta value ζ(2n+1) for n ≥ 2.
