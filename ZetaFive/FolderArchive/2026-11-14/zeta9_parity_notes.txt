ZETA(9) AND THE TWO PARITY QUESTIONS

The integration weight extends to s=9:
w9(y)=(2*pi)^8*y^9/20160 * sum_{l>=1} l^8 exp(-2*pi*l*y).
It is positive. For S_s(z)=sum_{n in Z}(z+n)^(-s),
w_s(y)=(-1)^((s-1)/2)*i*y^s*S_s(iy)/pi for odd s>=3.
The sign changes between residue classes 1 and 3 modulo 4, but it is
absorbed in the conversion from an imaginary row sum to a positive weight.
The full level-one odd-weight lattice sum cancels for every odd s.

There is a different, genuine modulo-four distinction in the periods of
even-weight Eisenstein series. The period construction for zeta(s) uses
weight s+1: E6 for zeta5, E8 for zeta7, E10 for zeta9.
Ramanujan's transformation at alpha=beta=pi contains the factor
1-(-1)^m, where s=2m+1. It vanishes for s=5,9 and survives for s=7.
This special-point cancellation does not obstruct the positive moment
representation, nor does it prove irrationality in the surviving case.
