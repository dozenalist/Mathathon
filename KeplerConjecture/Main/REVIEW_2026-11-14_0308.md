## Independent claim decisions

**K15-SUP-0308-01 — REFUTED_INFERENCE: Main C081's proposed transfer floor.**
The proof starts with `D = sum_C b <= A = 13*(sqrt32 - Vdod)` and uses the cut
requirement `D <= Phi*B` to infer `Phi >= A/B`. This does not follow: both
premises bound D from above. For example, D=0 and Phi=0 satisfy both inequalities
when A and B are positive. A necessary positive floor requires an actual lower
bound on D, or a configuration attaining the proposed deficit. No argument shows
all thirteen cells have volume Vdod. The claimed 0.0104949 floor and the rule
rejecting every smaller Phi are therefore **unproved and must not be used**.
This refutes the inference, not every possible independently justified floor.
The central singleton may yield a different valid necessary condition.

Main subsequently accepted this objection, withdrew C081, retained the rejected
argument and proposed C081r from the central singleton; that correction is now
merged at f1e4d8b4. Its routing document overgeneralizes the replacement floor:
a constant that must cover the icosahedral witness is different from the minimum
constant for every individual packing with some positive deficit. The immediate
per-singleton bound is b(v)/degree(v). Main and Computation received this scope
correction before any such fixture rejection rule was accepted.
