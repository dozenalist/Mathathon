1. INDEPENDENT PROOF OF THE PRIMARY RESULT

Let I be finite with measurable singleton fibres, let f:R->I be measurable,
and fix d in R. Write A_i={t:f(t)=i}, S_i=supp(volume restricted to A_i),
Q_ij=A_i intersect {t:f(t+d)=j}, and K_ij=supp(volume restricted to Q_ij).
Assume for every ordered pair that S_i intersect S_j nonempty implies
volume(Q_ij)=0. Then S_i intersect {a:a+d in S_i} is empty for every i.

Proof of projection. The Q_ij form a finite measurable partition of A_i.
Every K_ij is contained in S_i by monotonicity of restricted measures.
Conversely, if a is in no K_ij, choose an open neighbourhood U_j of a
with volume(U_j intersect Q_ij)=0 for each j. The FINITE intersection U
of these neighbourhoods is open and contains a, and
volume(U intersect A_i) <= sum_j volume(U_j intersect Q_ij)=0.
Thus a is not in S_i. Hence S_i=union_j K_ij, without taking a closure.

Proof of transport. If a is in K_ij and a+d is not in S_j, there is an
open neighbourhood V of a+d with volume(V intersect A_j)=0. Translation
preserves null sets, and Q_ij intersect (V-d) is contained in
(V intersect A_j)-d. This contradicts a in K_ij. Therefore a+d is in S_j.

Now suppose a and a+d both belong to S_i. The projection identity chooses
one j with a in K_ij. Transport gives a+d in S_j, so S_i and S_j meet.
The hypothesis makes Q_ij null and K_ij empty, contradiction.

This proof explicitly uses joint fibres, not an independent choice from
two marginal support lists. Its conclusion is POINTWISE. No density-point
upgrade of a pointwise level-set statement is being smuggled into it.

An equally valid and much smaller formal route already exists in the source.
Set R(i,j)=Disjoint(S_i,S_j). The contact hypothesis says that every pair
with not R(i,j) occurs on a null set. A finite union over ordered pairs gives
R(f(t),f(t+d)) almost everywhere. FiveSupport:55-69,
stateSupport_shift_relation, then gives j with R(i,j) and a+d in S_j
whenever a in S_i. Membership of a+d in S_i contradicts disjointness.
The existing lemma's proof uses a finite closed union and pullback of the
almost-everywhere support-membership statement by translation. Its exact
hypotheses fit the proposed use. No joint-tuple library is necessary.

5. SIX-COLOUR LOCAL ANNULUS — ACCEPTED WITH LOCAL SCOPE

For A={.99<|z|<1.01}, nine half-open sectors of angle 40 degrees, coloured
cyclically 0,1,2, form a proper colouring of the unit-distance pairs whose
two endpoints lie in A.

In one sector the angular separation is less than 40 degrees and
cos(delta)>2/3. Therefore squared distance is less than
(1/50)^2+(2/3)(101/100)^2=10207/15000<1.
Distinct sectors of the same colour have circular angular separation at
least 80 degrees, so cos(delta)<1/3 and squared distance is greater than
(4/3)(99/100)^2=3267/2500>1. Half-open endpoint conventions do not destroy
these strict margins. The trigonometric comparisons with 45 and 75
degrees stated by the lead are correct.

The claim about ACTUAL palettes is also sound. For |x|<1/200, the unit
circle at x lies a positive distance inside A. For every y_n->x and r_n->1,
all sufficiently late circles lie in A. The limiting circle meets each
sector boundary ray finitely often. Away from those finitely many angles,
all colour indicators on y_n+r_n e(t) converge pointwise to those on
x+e(t). Their boundedness gives strong L2 convergence. By
Compactness.trace_realization:262 every actual trace at x has precisely
this vector as its L2 value. Palettes.paletteSet_minimal:26 and
tracePositive_le_palette:23 then give the asserted singleton palette
almost everywhere. This argument handles the actual essential union of
ALL traces; it does not freely assign palette lists.

Consequently the same three labels hit the palette at every centre of an
open ball. For fixed x,z in that ball, every source-admissible smooth
transport branch has all three relevant points in A, and both relevant
target/source distances are one. Their singleton palettes are disjoint
almost everywhere along the branch. Source nonzero-derivative hypotheses
prevent a positive-measure parameter set from lying on the finitely many
exceptional angular rays. Sixty-degree disjointness also holds.

The eta,d example is correct. Set eta=pi/10000 and
d=2cos(pi/3-eta)-1. Then 0<d<2eta<1/200, and
e(eta)+e(2pi/3-eta)=(1+d)e(pi/3).
The source labels are 0 and 2; the target label is 1. These are interior
sector points. The sum-map Jacobian is nonzero. Varying the target angle
near pi/3 with fixed centre z=d e(pi/3) gives smooth source branches;
at the displayed point both source angular derivatives are 1/(1+d)>0.
Thus this is an admissible open-parameter obstruction to forcing equality
of the two source states from a ternary hitting palette.

The extension by a constant outside A is emphatically NOT WeakColoring.
For example, far from A both q and q+e(t) have that same constant label
for an open positive-area set of q and every t. The global density-unit
exclusion also fails at the two density points 10 and 11 on the real axis.
The construction refutes the asserted inference from the LOCAL transport
conclusions to ternary-centre separation/countability. It does not refute
T6 under the full global weak-colouring hypothesis.

6. ACTUAL LOCAL INTERFACES AND TRANSITIONS — ACCEPTED

Colour A with 3,4,5. For K=[0,rho v], |v|=1 and 0<rho<1/200,
the actual source Straddle definition at AngularBoundary:147 implies
1-rho<|z|<1+rho for every z in Straddle(K). Hence Straddle(K) lies in A.
The spatial supports of labels 0,1,2 can all be excluded from it. The
segment supplies the actual ApproachingContinuum data at 0. Thus all
three endpoint exclusions of a common-interface triangle are present.
CommonInterface itself does not assert a transition edge, and it is
important not to conflate these objects.

The additional inner-disk construction supplies actual transition edges
as well. In B(0,rho), insert disjoint disks with centres
q_n=rho*2^(-n), n>=1, radii rho*2^(-n-3), and three 120-degree sectors
labelled 0,1,2 in each disk. The stated disks are disjoint and contained
in B(0,rho). Their union with A is properly six-coloured: the inner disk
has diameter below one, and its labels differ from every annular label.

For each q_n, each label pair meets on an interface ray. In every ball
B(q_n,R), choose a positive-length interface segment away from the vertex,
other rays and the disk boundary. A narrow rectangle about it has only
the two relevant colours. Translating normally by h produces transition
area at least L|h| for some fixed L>0 and all sufficiently small h along
that normal direction. This proves positive limsup of transitionRatio
under the exact punctured-neighbourhood definition in Transitions:21-24.
It is stronger and more precise than just saying the ratio fails to tend
to zero. Thus every q_n has the transition triangle. Closedness puts the
same edges at 0. All nearby-centre unit-circle palettes still come from
A and are singleton almost everywhere.

This is a valid local realization of accumulating transition triangles,
not a counterexample to L6 for global weak colourings. The spatial-support
exclusions for the short radial continua survive adding the inner disk.

7. SIX-POSITION CONSTRAINTS AND THE NEXT TARGET

AngularBoundary:259 and :279 give intersections of palettes with the two
allowed label sets, not inclusion of a palette in both sets. The packet's
R5 correctly encodes these statements. Its R6 follows because an entire
cycle edge is excluded from the allowed set on the side of that edge.
No TransitionEdge premise is needed for this finite deduction.

My independent checker accepted the four stated m=3,4,5,6 certificates.
It reconstructed each sign as sign(cos((j-q_i)pi/3)), checked the opposite
rows, nonempty palettes of size at most two, successive disjointness,
both allowed-set intersections, and noncontainment in each cycle edge.
There is no orthogonality at the six sampled positions. Strict signs
persist on a neighbourhood of each sampled base angle, so the geometric
sign certificate is not an isolated exceptional-angle artefact.

These witnesses reject ONLY 'R1-R6 imply no cycle'. They omit actual trace
compatibility across base angles, support closure and general transports
between centres. They cannot establish SAT for a global weak colouring.

States.two_state_impossible:243-307 is indeed generic in k. In the m=6
witness J={0,2}, K={3,5}, the omitted labels are {1,4}. Any measurable
whole-circle Boolean state represented by these J,K and flipping at pi/3
would satisfy its cardinality premise and be impossible under the actual
global weak-colouring hypothesis. Its proof crucially also uses the
triangleAngle shift, density transport and a unit triangle; the six fixed
orbit positions contain no substitute for that coupling.

It is therefore sensible to test whether the full six-cycle interface
data force such a global two-state representation. The finite witness
does NOT prove that reduction, and the theorem cannot be applied just
because one six-position word alternates two palettes. A surviving
multi-state whole-circle field would also have to satisfy all represented
state and transport premises. This is an appropriate bounded next target,
not a positive six-colour result.

The conditional roadmap separates two genuine obligations: local
finiteness of transition cycles (L6), and acyclicity of actual common
interfaces (F6). The source supports their distinct roles. Once suitable
finite-label generalizations are proved, L6 supplies the finite core and
F6 supplies forest fillings. Five:22-100 and Mesh:92-149 then use graph
realizations, finite coordinates, connectedness and unit-distance average
exclusion, without a five-label enumeration in that final obstruction.
However the current Lean declarations are still specialized to Fin 5.
Calling their generalization plausible implementation work is reasonable;
calling the entire six-colour chain already formalized would be false.
No implication from B2 alone to either L6 or F6 has been proved here.
