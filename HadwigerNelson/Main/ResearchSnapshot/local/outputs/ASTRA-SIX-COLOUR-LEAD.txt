ASTRA SIX-COLOUR LEAD — FIRST SUBSTANTIVE PACKET
14 November 2026. Source-checked mathematics; no claimed solution of six versus seven.

DECISION

The proposed direct ternary analogue of binary-centre separation has a real
local geometric obstruction. A thin annulus has a proper three-colouring
by nine sectors; the actual palettes at every nearby centre are singleton
and hit the same triple. Hence ternary centres fill an OPEN BALL in a local
model satisfying the exact two-centre unit-distance transport conclusions.
The global weak-colouring hypothesis fails outside the annulus, so the full
ternary-centre conjecture is not disproved. A successful proof must use
global information that the binary separation mechanism does not need.

There are also two independent downstream obligations: local finiteness of
transition cycles, and exclusion of common-interface cycles for core filling.
Even granting palette size <=2, the source-derived six-position interface
relaxations have exact models for EACH cycle length 3,4,5,6. These models
refute a finite combinatorial shortcut, not the stronger analytic statements.

Recommendation: do not spend the next block seeking a one-lemma completion
by generic ternary separation. Give the solving team one bounded global
interface program: identify and test the missing analytic coupling beyond
the locally realizable annulus and beyond the six-position strip rules.
The most useful source-driven target is a specific six-colour common-interface
cycle obstruction (or a sharply delimited obstruction to it), incorporating
the already generic States.two_state_impossible before inventing new finite
constraints. Finite-core regularity alone still cannot finish the argument.

2. THE PRECISE TERNARY TARGET AND ITS ACTUAL CONSUMER

Let C={0,...,5}, e(t)=exp(it), and P_x(t) be the ACTUAL palette in the source:
the essential union of positive-coordinate sets of all limiting typical
circle traces at centre x. Compactness.lean:261 and Palettes.lean:10-40
specify these objects. They are not arbitrary independently chosen lists.

For fixed S subset C, |S|=3, define
  T_S(y) : for angular-almost every u, P_y(u) intersects S.
The proposed lemma is
  T6: for EVERY x in C-plane and EVERY three-element S subset C,
      for angular-almost every t, not T_S(x+e(t)).
Its hypotheses are c:C-plane->Fin 6, Measurable c, WeakColoring 6 c.
The exceptional set may depend on x,S. No simultaneous conull set over
uncountably many centres is asserted or needed for the following deduction.

For each fixed x, BinaryPalettes.lean:211-233 gives, for a.e. t, both:
  (i) for a.e. u, c(x+e(t)+e(u)) is not in P_x(t);
  (ii) TypicalSampling c (x+e(t)) 1.
The typical sampling palette inclusion says that, for a.e. u, that actual
sampled colour belongs to P_(x+e(t))(u). If a fixed three-element A is
contained in P_x(t), all those colours lie in S=C minus A. Thus T_S(x+e(t)).
T6 excludes this. There are only finitely many A, so intersect their conull
sets. Together with palette_nonempty this proves
  B2: for every x, for a.e. t, 1 <= |P_x(t)| <= 2.
This is a complete valid implication T6 => B2.

The same existing binary argument at k=6 already excludes four-element
palettes, since their complements have two elements. Thus |P|<=3 is a
routine source consequence. It is useful preparation, not a breakthrough.

Countability of each T_S-centre set would suffice for T6 because t->x+e(t)
is injective and angular Haar measure has no atoms. Uniform separation
would suffice for countability. The annulus below defeats a proof of either
of these stronger statements from the LOCAL transport conclusions alone.

3. COMPLETE CONDITIONAL CHAIN TO A SIX-COLOUR CONTRADICTION

There is no established arrow from B2 to either L6 or F6 below.

L6, transition-core obligation:
  Under measurable weak six-colouring, for every compact K, the set of
  x in K where the ACTUAL transition graph Gamma_x has a cycle is finite.
At k=5 this is CyclicCore.lean:269. It assembles 3-,4-,5-cycle cases via
permutations. At k=6 one must handle all lengths 3,4,5,6. Triangle centres
at k=5 are binary centres because B2 plus avoiding each triangle edge
forces hitting the TWO outsiders (Accumulation.lean:157). At k=6 it forces
hitting THREE outsiders, and B2 alone gives no isolation. The four-/five-
cycle classifications also change: there are respectively two/one outside
labels at six colours, and a new spanning six-cycle. The existing five-
colour finite classifications cannot simply be retyped.

Given L6, choose a finite set L covering the cyclic core in the radius-8
disk. The proof of HoleApproximation.lean:115,291 constructs continuous
graph-valued maps outside tiny core holes, agreeing with the thresholded
disk-average map away from all exceptional disks. Its theorem hypotheses
are measurability, a positive threshold alpha<1/5, and that finite-core
condition; weak colouring and B2 are not additional inputs there. At six
replace alpha<1/5 by alpha<1/6. Generalizing the finite label type and the
associated finite sums is implementation work, not a new analytic theorem.

F6, common-interface obligation:
  For every x, the graph on six labels whose edge ij has a common
  approaching continuum K with BOTH spatialSupport(c,i) and
  spatialSupport(c,j) disjoint from Straddle(K) is acyclic.
The continuum approaches the SAME x in a unit direction. Different edges
may use different continua and directions. There is no assertion that a
CommonInterface is the same object as a TransitionEdge.
At k=5 this is ContinuumLimits.lean:296, proved using separate triangle,
four-cycle and five-cycle exclusion arguments. Six colours need lengths
3 through 6. B2 does not supply these exclusions; see the certificates below.

Given F6, actual compact midpoint bridges in annuli produce common
interfaces in a limit: ContinuumLimits.lean:318. This compactness and support
exclusion step is colour-uniform in substance (its core support-exclusion
theorem at :200 is already generic in k). F6 then makes sufficiently fine
crossing graphs forests (FullApproximation.lean:12,64). The forest-extension
theorem fills all core disks; finite pasting preserves agreement outside
the disks (FullApproximation.lean:175,206). Together with L6 this gives
arbitrarily fine FullApprox_6 (the analogue of :268,330).

Precisely, for fixed alpha in (0,1/6) and every t>0 it produces epsilon in
(0,t), finitely many disjoint exceptional closed disks with total radii<t,
and a continuous F:closedBall(0,5)->|K_6| agreeing outside these disks with
the normalized positive parts max(diskAverage_i-alpha,0).

Such FullApprox_6 is impossible. Every point in a graph realization has
at most two positive coordinates, so the open components of its positive
coordinate sets form a cover of multiplicity at most two. The planar
covering obstruction forces a positive-coordinate connected set of
diameter>2 (Five.lean:22,94). Applying this at t_n->0, taking a repeatedly
occurring label, and using Mesh.lean:92 rules out that sequence. That mesh
argument uses unit-pair disk averages, vanishing exceptional annuli,
compactness and connectedness; it does not use a five-label enumeration.

Therefore the honest sufficient chain is
  proper six-colouring -> measurable weak six-colouring
  + L6 + F6 -> FullApprox_6 -> planar-cover/mesh contradiction.
T6 => B2 is a proposed route toward parts of L6/F6, not a replacement for
them. Even a direct proof of both L6 and F6 would not need T6 as a separate
input. At present this route has multiple independent hard gaps.

4. A GENUINE LOCAL GEOMETRIC COUNTERMODEL TO TERNARY SEPARATION

Let A={z:99/100<|z|<101/100}. Partition its angles into nine half-open
sectors I_j=[2*pi*j/9,2*pi*(j+1)/9), j=0,...,8. Give sector I_j colour
j mod 3. Extend measurably outside A by colour 3, viewed in Fin 6.
This extension is NOT a weak colouring of the whole plane.

Claim A: all unit-distance pairs contained in A have different colours.
For points with radii r,s and circular angular separation delta,
  |z-w|^2=(r-s)^2+2*r*s*(1-cos(delta)).
If they are in the same sector, delta<40 degrees and cos(delta)>2/3:
  |z-w|^2 < (1/50)^2 + (2/3)*(101/100)^2
            = 10207/15000 < 1.
If they are in distinct same-colour sectors, delta>=80 degrees and
cos(delta)<1/3:
  |z-w|^2 > (4/3)*(99/100)^2 = 3267/2500 > 1.
The trigonometric bounds follow from 40<45 and 80>75 degrees,
cos45>2/3, cos75<1/3. The latter radical inequality follows from
sqrt(3)>14/9. Half-open boundaries obey the same strict distance margins;
there is no hidden monochromatic unit pair on a sector boundary.

Claim B: for every centre x with |x|<1/200, P_x(t) is the singleton
{c(x+e(t))} for angular-a.e. t, and hence T_{0,1,2}(x) holds.
All unit circles and sufficiently close sampling circles around such x
remain inside A. The limiting circle meets each radial sector boundary
in finitely many points. Away from those angles, c(y_n+r_n*e(t)) converges
pointwise to c(x+e(t)) whenever y_n->x and r_n->1. Dominated convergence
gives strong L2 convergence of every colour indicator. Therefore every
actual source trace has this same indicator vector, by trace_realization
(Compactness.lean:262) and uniqueness of its weak limit. Palette minimality
and existence/nonemptiness yield the claimed singleton palette a.e.
This checks the REAL source trace definition, not just arbitrary lists.

Claim C: for all fixed x,z in this small ball and every admissible smooth
transport branch z+e(a(r))=x+e(s(r))+e(t(r)), the source conclusions
P_z(a(r)) disjoint P_x(s(r)) and P_z(a(r)) disjoint P_x(t(r)) hold a.e.
All three relevant points are in A and target-source distances are exactly
one. Claim A gives the inequalities; the branch nondegeneracy hypotheses
preserve the null sets in Claim B. The same argument gives the sixty-degree
palette disjointness at every such centre. Consequently all these exact
local transport rules coexist with an open ball of ternary centres.

The failure occurs at the central binary inference, before dynamics.
Let eta=pi/10000, d=2*cos(pi/3-eta)-1, x=0, z=d*e(pi/3).
Then 0<d<2*eta<1/200, and
  e(eta)+e(2*pi/3-eta)=z+e(pi/3).
The two source endpoints have colours 0 and 2, while the target has colour
1. All points are in the interiors of their sectors. Thus the geometry
persists on an open set of parameter values: this is not a null-angle
exception. The target avoids both source colours without forcing the two
source states to agree. BinaryPalettes:76-115 uses exactly that agreement
for a TWO-label hitting cover. Three labels remove it.

This local model also shows why the common-interface triangle argument is
different at six colours. Relabel the annulus colours as 3,4,5. Put any
three short radial continua K_i=[0,rho*v_i], 0<rho<1/200, at the origin.
Their straddling sets lie in A, so both endpoint spatial supports of every
triangle edge among 0,1,2 are excluded there. Thus actual local triangle
interface data and singleton palettes coexist. Outside A the extension
again violates WeakColoring. A global input is indispensable.

One can also include ACTUAL accumulating transition triangles in this local
model, rather than merely naming edges. In B(0,rho), use colours 0,1,2;
keep the annulus coloured 3,4,5. Their union is properly six-coloured:
the inner disk has diameter<1 and its labels are disjoint from the annulus
labels. Choose disjoint disks centred at q_n=rho*2^(-n), n>=1, of radii
rho*2^(-n-3). Partition each into three 120-degree sectors with labels
0,1,2; fill the remainder of the inner disk with 0. Every q_n has all
three transition edges in the SOURCE definition. In every ball about q_n,
a short piece of each interface ray has an adjacent two-colour strip;
translations normal to that ray give transition area at least a positive
constant times the translation length. Hence the ratio cannot tend to
zero. The q_n accumulate at 0; transition-edge closedness also puts the
same triangle at 0. Palettes at all centres |x|<1/200 still come solely
from the annulus and are singleton a.e. This does not violate L6 because
the global extension is not weak. It shows that even actual local
transition abundance plus local unit exclusions do not force L6.

Grades: claims A-C and the explicit transport failure have a complete
paper-level argument here; their rational constants/finite sector incidence
are machine checked. This is not Lean-checked and not an independent referee
verdict. It refutes the named local implication, not T6 with its full global
hypotheses, and not the existence/nonexistence of a six-colouring.
