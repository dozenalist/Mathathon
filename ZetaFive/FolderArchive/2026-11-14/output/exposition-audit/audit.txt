EXPOSITION AND ATTRIBUTION AUDIT
14 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles

Conclusion

No confirmed unattributed extended verbatim passage was found in the public sources examined. This is not an originality certificate. The PDFs explicitly credit their main mathematical sources, Chun Wing Lai and Aabir Fauzan. However, the references omit an exact earlier mathematical antecedent (Marc Prevost's Stieltjes representation and moments) and a particularly close related exposition (Persiflage). Those should be acknowledged before the drafts are circulated as a polished exposition. Similarity does not establish that either source was actually copied or consulted.

The strongest conclusion is therefore: credited reconstruction of Lai/Fauzan, with incomplete historical and related-exposition references; no demonstrated textual plagiarism in this search.

Priority 3: Add full bibliographic entries for the classical formulas actually used and for Andreief's identity. Add Zudilin/Brown in a short historical note if the intended scope extends beyond an exposition of Lai. Add a direct Fauzan link to A.

There is no need to cite every merely similar Hexagon paper or every work involving a Gram matrix. Those items document search coverage and context; they are not all missing mandatory references.

Method, negative results and limits

Three parallel searches covered distinctive prose, arXiv/primary antecedents, and Hexagon/ai.vixra. A dedicated phrase pass tested 20 distinctive quoted phrases, eight broader or near-phrase queries and one shortened follow-up. Additional mathematical and domain-restricted queries are recorded in the accompanying logs. No extended exact wording match was confirmed for the sampled distinctive sentences. Search engines sometimes relaxed quotes and returned unrelated matches; those were not treated as matches or as evidence of absence.

An exploratory local normalized-text comparison was also performed against Lai/Fauzan. Formula tokens and PDF spacing/font artifacts limit such comparisons; its results are not a plagiarism score or an originality test. The conclusions rely on source reading, visible credits, and the specific comparisons above.

Hexagon's direct search endpoint and one ai.vixra abstract endpoint were unavailable to the web tool. Domain-restricted web search and explicit primary PDF links worked. Unindexed PDFs, paywalled/unavailable material, changed pages, books, and close paraphrases can be missed. The displayed dates of blog posts do not establish the dates of all subsequent edits. No finite search can find literally everything online or prove a text was never influenced by another source.

This is an attribution audit, not legal advice, a proof-validity certification, or a complete priority history. The useful action is to improve the references above while preserving the distinction between exact mathematical antecedents, related exposition, and demonstrated textual borrowing.
