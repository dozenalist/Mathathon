1. THE SINGLE CLOSED SET

For fixed measurable c:C->Fin k and a label set K, define
  Z_K = {y in C : there exists beta in TraceVector k,
         IsTrace c hc y beta and traceValue(beta,i)=0 for every i in K}.
Crucially this is a set in the PLANE depending only on c and K, not on a
base centre or an angular state set.

Z_K is closed. To prove sequential closedness, let y_n->y and choose a
K-avoiding trace beta_n at y_n. Compactness of TraceVector gives a
subsequence beta_{phi(n)}->beta in the weak trace topology.
Compactness.lean:289 trace_limit, using isClosed_traceGraph:284, gives
IsTrace at y. For i in K, test this weak convergence against traceValue
beta i itself. Every inner product along the sequence is zero; the limit
is <traceValue beta i,traceValue beta i>, hence traceValue beta i=0.
Thus y belongs to Z_K. C is metrizable, so sequential closedness suffices.
One may instead use that the projection of a closed subset of
C x TraceVector along the compact second factor is closed.

This is NOT a claim that arbitrary angular state supports vary upper
semicontinuously with the base centre. The avoiding-trace set is a larger
closed set that contains the relevant points, which is exactly what the
argument needs.

3. THREE-CENTRE EXCLUSION ON Z_K

Suppose A is a positive-measure RealState at x represented by J and the
complement of J union K is exactly {X}. Then
  y,z in Z_K, |y-x|=|z-x|=1 ==> |y-z| != 1.               (E)

Proof: select the K-avoiding traces at y,z. They imply P_y,P_z are not
subsets of K for almost every common angular parameter t. Neighbor
palette transport makes P_x,P_y,P_z pairwise disjoint if all three
distances equal one. Pull the five conull statements to R. On the
positive-measure A, J is contained in P_x, so each of P_y,P_z must contain
the unique colour X outside J union K. Contradiction. The full proof and
exact exceptional-set contract are in a2-fixed-state-proof.txt.

Thus Z_K intersect the unit circle at x is a compact unit-distance-free
set whenever the indicated positive complementary state exists at x.
This is a geometric property of one global closed set, not a finite
selection from changing angular supports.

4. CONVERGING-CENTRE THEOREM

Fix measurable weak k-colouring c, label sets J,K with outside label X.
Assume:
  x_n -> x in C;
  for every n, B_n is a RealState at x_n represented by K;
  A is a RealState at x represented by J, and volume A>0;
  b_n in supp(volume|B_n), d_n in supp(volume|B_n);
  b_n -> b and d_n -> d in R.
Then |direction(b)-direction(d)| != 1.

Proof. By (S), y_n=x_n+direction(b_n) and z_n=x_n+direction(d_n) belong
to Z_K. They converge to y=x+direction(b), z=x+direction(d). Closedness
places y,z in Z_K. Both are at distance one from x. Apply (E). QED.

The two avoiding traces at the limits may be obtained from DIFFERENT
subsequences. No simultaneous realization is required: each is an actual
trace at its stated limit centre, and the source palette is the essential
union of all trace-positive supports. The source neighbor-palette theorem
already applies to these two entire palettes. After choosing the two
traces, only a finite intersection of their conull constraints is used.
This is different from incorrectly selecting unrelated angular state
values and claiming they form one joint word.

Compared with the original generic strict-state sequence theorem, this
contract ADDS convergence x_n->x and a positive represented J-state AT x.
It REMOVES all A_n and their local L1 convergence assumptions. We do not
claim these contracts equivalent in general. The new contract is exactly
available at the actual four-state consumer.

9. THE GENERAL FINITE-GRAPH FORM

The elementary forcing argument has a useful formulation for any k.
Let L=Fin k\(J union K), and suppose a positive RealState at x represents
J. For every FINITE family y_v of points in Z_K with |y_v-x|=1, the
unit-distance graph on those points has a proper colouring by labels in L.

Proof. Choose a K-avoiding trace at every y_v. For almost every common
angular t, each P_{y_v}(t) is not a subset of K, is disjoint from P_x(t),
and neighboring P_{y_v}(t),P_{y_w}(t) are disjoint. The finiteness of the
vertex and edge sets allows a single conull intersection. Choose a real
t in the positive J-state satisfying all these conditions. Every
P_{y_v}(t) intersects L; choose a label from that intersection. Adjacent
vertices cannot receive the same label, by palette disjointness. QED.

For |L|=1 this is exactly (E): there can be no unit edge. Thus the fixed
and converging four/five support exclusions are consequences of one
closed-set / finite-graph transfer principle.
For |L|=2 it yields only bipartiteness of finite unit graphs on this one
circle. Such graphs are already bipartite: each orbit of angular rotation
by pi/3 is a 6-cycle, and unit chords connect successive orbit points.
Consequently the one-circle residual-colour argument by itself supplies
no analogous six-colour contradiction when two residual labels remain.
This is a precise limitation of the general principle, not a claim about
the full six-colour problem or about transports between different circles.
