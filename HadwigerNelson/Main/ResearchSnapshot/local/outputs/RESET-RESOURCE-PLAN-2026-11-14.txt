RESET DECISION RULE: PREPARE AT 30%, REDEEM AT A VERIFIED NEED
The following thresholds are operational recommendations, not claims about platform eligibility.
1. Before a substantial tranche, inspect the applicable live meter and reset inventory. Verify covered window(s), plan, scope, expiry, redemption conditions, and whether consuming it shifts the next natural reset. Meter must be fresh within 10 minutes; a stale or unavailable read means UNKNOWN, not zero. Prepare a 60–120-minute worthwhile task with a concrete artifact and review path.
2. At <=30% remaining in a relevant window, prepare the next reset decision and reduce duplicate context; do not reduce useful research merely because the warning threshold was crossed.
3. At <=15%, admit one bounded task at a time unless measured comparable tasks show that a pair fits. Use the maximum observed allowance change across the last two comparable tasks, plus 50% headroom, as a rough admission estimate. If estimates are unavailable, use a single pilot task. Team-wide deltas are noisy and must not be presented as isolated task costs.
4. Redeem ONE verified eligible saved reset when (a) a binding covered allowance is <=10% or exhausted, (b) a prepared valuable tranche needs that allowance, and (c) the natural refresh is more than 45 minutes away or waiting would materially endanger review/cutoff. If the next natural refresh is within 45 minutes, use another provider's independent ready task or finish source/compute work meanwhile. The parent can choose earlier eligible MANUAL redemption for an expiring reset or a task demonstrably too large for the remaining window; record why. Do not evade an assistant eligibility gate through another route.
5. After redemption, refresh once after normal propagation and confirm what changed. If uncertain, inspect history/state rather than consuming a second reset as a test. Recalculate the next refresh time from the UI, not the old schedule. Admit the already-prepared tranche. Re-evaluate each later reset separately.
6. Use earlier reset capacity when it buys an extra completed, reviewed daytime tranche. Preserve capacity for one final referee through ordinary remaining allowance, an independently metered provider, or a verified unspent reset. This is a functional coverage requirement, not a fixed percentage hoard or a ban on spending the last reset on a valuable earlier task.
