INDEPENDENT AC REFEREE — 14 NOVEMBER 2026

VERDICT

ACCEPT AC at paper level. The aligned-continuum data really do contradict
measurable WeakColoring when their excluded labels leave at most two
colours. The critical binary-strip lemma permits U=V, requires no
disjointness of the two open regions, and explicitly allows independent
inner/outer choices. Its actual proof supports those advertised hypotheses.
No missing mathematical premise or counterexample was found.

ACCEPT its stated six-colour consumer: three consecutive cycle edges
with one common ORIENTED approach direction exclude their four endpoint
labels, leaving two. In particular AC excludes actual witness data with
q=(0,3,3,3,0,0), using edges 1,2,3 and direction -1.

ACCEPT NR and TC as the stated null-return/necessary-condition theorems.
They do not establish impossibility of every positive-measure partial
two-state field. The four-state and near-full-measure constructions are
valid ANGULAR countermodels to the stronger recurrence inference, not
actual global colourings.

No six-colour impossibility, general nonaligned interface exclusion,
novelty claim, or fresh Lean pass follows from this review. Private
formal validation of AC and its exact consumer is warranted.

1. PRECISE GENERIC AC CONTRACT

A convenient exact formulation is as follows. Let c:C->Fin k be measurable
and WeakColoring k c. Fix a finite index set I, a centre x, a vector v with
norm v=1, sets K_i and finite label sets H_i. Assume
  ApproachingContinuum x K_i v for every i;
  Disjoint(spatialSupport c j, Straddle K_i) for every j in H_i;
  fixed a,b:Fin k such that every label other than a,b belongs to some H_i.
Then False.

The last assumption can equivalently be obtained from a residual label
set of cardinality at most two. The pair may repeat. If the residual set
is empty, choose any padding colour, available as c(0); if it is a
singleton, repeat that colour. No k>=2 assumption is necessary. The
empty-family case, if admitted, reduces to the impossibility of the
corresponding globally at-most-two-coloured weak map; empty finite minima
can simply be replaced by any positive width.

The proof does not need a palette-cardinality bound, transition edges,
cyclic-core regularity, FourCenter or FiveCenter. It uses the source's
actual spatial supports and actual traces. The approach direction must
be the same vector, not merely the same unoriented line. Opposite vectors
may demand opposite radial sides at one angle, so their strips cannot be
intersected in the same way. Approximate alignment is not covered here.

2. OPEN REGION AND UNIFORM LOCAL STRIPS

Put W=intersection_i Straddle(K_i). AngularBoundary:147-155 makes each
Straddle open for arbitrary K_i, so W is open by finiteness. The support
exclusions give, for plane-a.e. z in W, c(z) outside every H_i, hence
c(z)=a or c(z)=b. This is exactly finite_support_exclusion_ae:317-327,
followed by the finite label-cover assumption. It does not presume that
pointwise colour classes equal their essential spatial supports.

For a unit vector e with nonzero inner product against v, all K_i provide
the SAME radial side:
  positive inner product: outer strip, AngularBoundary:244-250;
  negative inner product: inner strip, AngularBoundary:251-257.
Each strip has a positive width controlling both angular distance of e'
from e and radial distance from one. A finite positive minimum gives a
whole one-sided strip contained in W. No width uniform over all angles
is required. The conversions to the binary-strip interface are valid:
  |r-1|<delta and r>1 imply r<1+delta;
  |r-1|<delta and r<1 imply r>1-delta;
and the required r>0 is retained explicitly.
Sectors.ApproachingContinuum.side_strip:16-29 already performs precisely
these conversions with a Boolean choosing the radial side.

Let B={t: inner product(direction t,v)=0}. Since v is unit, B consists of
the two directions Iv and -Iv, represented by an angle beta and beta+pi.
The direction map is injective on Angles. Hence B is a measurable null
set of cardinality two. No additional exceptional geometric directions
are needed for this strip-production step.

3. BINARYCENTER MEANS A HITTING PAIR

At each t outside B, outer_strip_trace:182-209 or
inner_strip_trace:210-239 produces an open angular neighbourhood on which
P_x(u) meets {a,b} almost everywhere. The actual theorem does NOT say
P_x(u) is contained in {a,b}. The proposed argument uses only intersection,
which is correct.

PaletteTransport.ae_of_locally_ae:258 supplies the needed countable-cover
gluing on the second-countable angular circle. Combining it with the
nullity of B yields BinaryCenter c hc x a b as defined in
BinaryPalettes:9-10. This is not an intersection of uncountably many
conull sets and does not require selecting one trace for every angle on
a common exceptional set.

BinaryPalettes.binary_alternation:11-27 applies at arbitrary k. It gives
both
  a in P_x(t+tau) iff a notin P_x(t),
  b in P_x(t) iff a notin P_x(t)
almost everywhere, where tau=pi/3. Thus exactly one of a,b occurs almost
everywhere; other colours may also occur and are irrelevant. If a=b,
binary_pair_distinct:28-37 already contradicts BinaryCenter.

Define s(t)=false when a is in P_x(t), and true otherwise. PaletteSet
measurability makes s measurable, and alternation gives s(t+tau)=not s(t)
almost everywhere. Nothing asserts that s records the entire palette.

4. THE CRITICAL U=V=W APPLICATION IS VALID

BoundaryTrace.binary_strip_palette_rigidity:280-290 has EXACTLY these
relevant premises:
  arbitrary k, measurable c, WeakColoring k c;
  two open regions U,V, each a.e. coloured from the SAME pair a,b;
  one positive common width;
  independently specified booleans bu,bv;
  a bu-side strip near direction t in U;
  a bv-side strip near direction t*exp(i*pi/3) in V.
There is NO premise U!=V, Disjoint U V, bu=bv, or bu!=bv. Therefore
U=V=W is a direct legitimate specialization.

For t and t+tau outside B, obtain their separate strips in W and shrink
to the minimum of the two widths. The identity
direction(t+tau)=direction(t)*exp(i*pi/3), stated at Sectors:152-156,
matches the second-strip premise exactly. Choose bu and bv from their
respective inner-product signs, independently.

I checked the proof rather than relying only on its signature:

* Rigidity.radial_unit_contact:261-309 shows that the unit circle centred
  at x+e enters EITHER prescribed side near x+e*exp(i*pi/3). Its explicit
  parameterization is radius 2cos(theta), direction e*exp(i*theta),
  with theta on the chosen side of pi/3. The displacement from x+e is
  e*exp(2i*theta), of norm one.
* Openness of V then gives two nonparallel unit vectors from that first
  endpoint into V, via open_two_unit_directions:226-244.
* A small connected polar box on the bu side lies in U and in the region
  where both translated endpoints lie in V. The first endpoint x+e
  itself need not belong to U. polarBox_small and polarBox_contains_strip
  explicitly handle this boundary-point issue.
* binary_open_local_rigidity:179-206 uses genuine four-edge paths, two
  colours, and a.e. inequalities for fixed unit directions. Its Fin 5
  indexing is the FIVE PATH VERTICES, not a five-colour restriction.
  U and V may overlap or coincide throughout this construction.
* connected_local_ae_constant fixes one essential colour on the connected
  polar box. One-sided weak-trace production then puts this fixed colour
  in P_x(u) a.e. on an angular neighbourhood of t.

Accordingly, for every t with t,t+tau outside B, a fixed i in {a,b} occurs
locally a.e. in the palette. If i=a, s is locally essentially false.
If i=b, the second equivalence in binary_alternation makes s locally
essentially true. One intersects the local conull statement with this
one global conull equivalence; no stronger palette containment is used.

5. THE BOUNDARY COUNT IS POINTWISE AND HAS THE CORRECT SHIFT

angleEssential s b, AngularBoundary:334-335, is exactly the existence of
an open neighbourhood on which s=b almost everywhere. The preceding
step therefore proves, for EVERY t outside
  F=B union {t:t+tau belongs to B}=B union (B-tau),
that t belongs to one of the two essential-constancy regions. Hence
  D=angleBoundary s is a subset of F POINTWISE,
not merely almost everywhere. The sign of the shifted exceptional set
is minus tau because it is the preimage of B under t->t+tau.
F has at most four points.

The source boundary argument applies without a finite-boundary assumption:
angleBoundary_nonempty:381-407 uses connectedness of the angular circle,
disjoint open essential-constancy regions, and the almost-everywhere flip
to prove D nonempty. angleBoundary_shift:370-380 upgrades the a.e. flip
to exact invariance of D under tau. angleBoundary_six:435-443 gives the
entire six-point orbit of any point of D, and sixAngle_injective:423-429
makes those six points distinct. Six points cannot fit into F. This proves
AC. There is no limit, measurable-selection, or uncountable-null-set gap
in this last step.

The proof keeps the actual global weak-colouring inputs behind the binary
transport and local rigidity theorems. It is not a consequence of assigning
arbitrary palette lists satisfying only the finite strip-side intersections.
Weak trace production, the existing palette transport analysis and the
measure-theoretic fixed-direction unit inequalities remain source inputs.

6. EXACT SIX-COLOUR CONSUMER AND ITS SCOPE

For cycle labels modulo six, edges E1={1,2}, E2={2,3}, E3={3,4} exclude
{1,2,3,4}. If their ACTUAL common-exclusion continua at the same centre
all approach in direction -1, AC applies with residual pair {0,5}.
This is exactly the selected subfamily in q=(0,3,3,3,0,0).
Relabelling the cycle or rotating all directions preserves the argument.

The original Lean CommonInterface declaration is specialized to Fin 5;
one should instantiate AC from the six-colour witness data directly, or
introduce a generic interface definition, rather than pretend that this
old declaration already has k=6 as a parameter. This is an implementation
typing issue, not a missing premise in the stated mathematical consumer.

For the four-state angular model P=(02,03,35,25) in pi/6 sectors, its
choice from {0,5} jumps from 5 to 0 at t=0. Both 0 and tau are outside
the perpendicular set for v=-1, so AC's two-strip step would force
essential constancy on a neighbourhood of zero. The jump occurs on
positive-measure intervals on both sides and cannot be repaired by
changing the value at zero. Thus the model cannot be realized by actual
weak-colouring common-interface data with this exact geometry.

No general six-cycle exclusion follows: nonaligned witness directions
remain outside this proof. In particular, finite sign lists alone do not
authorize this new constraint unless the common continuum, spatial-support
exclusion, common-centre and aligned-direction producers are retained.

7. SECONDARY NR/TC AUDIT

NR is valid. For a positive-measure same-phase gamma return in B_b,
the a.e. tau-swapping law produces, for j=0,1,2, memberships in B_b at
t+2j*tau and in B_(1-b) at t+(2j+3)*tau+gamma. Differentiate the ACTUAL
restricted state sets, using PaletteDensity.ae_lineDensityZero_compl:409,
and remove only finitely many translated exceptional sets. The resulting
six density memberships let States.states_densityOne:106-123 force the
same residual set outside J union K at all three sourceTriangle points.
The angle difference pi+gamma is nonparallel. sourceTriangle_pair:343
gives their unit triangle, and two_label_triangle_impossible:198 excludes
residual cardinality at most two. Thus each same-phase return set is null.
Disjointness of B0,B1 is not needed for this deduction itself.

One formal precision is worth retaining: quasiMeasurePreserving alone
does not imply that the preimage of a positive-measure set has positive
measure. For a contradiction based on positive ANGULAR return measure,
use the source angle_preimage_positive / fundamental-domain measure
preservation, or prove the real-a.e. statement first and transfer it to
angles using ae_angular_of_ae_line. The necessary source facts exist; this
does not add a mathematical hypothesis.

TC is also valid. For a finite measurable represented state map with
f(t+2tau)=f(t) a.e., remove all fibre-density exceptions and the finitely
many shifts needed for the three endpoint pairs. The first three endpoints
then share f(t), and the other three share f(t+pi+gamma). If their common
residual label set had cardinality at most two, the same triangle argument
would contradict it. Hence its size is at least three a.e. For actual
120-degree-periodic palettes, finite palette fibres are measurable and
represent their own labels, giving the stated union-size bound. A generic
finite-palette measurability proof may need to be written: the existing
Accumulation.palette_eq_measurable declaration is typed for Fin 5, though
its finite-intersection proof generalizes directly.

NR does NOT imply measure(B0 union B1)=0. The whole-circle theorem invokes
equal_triangleAngle_positive:291, which uses ergodicity of rotation by
2gamma. A partial set provides no gamma relation when either endpoint
falls into a gap; ordinary recurrence at some multiple of gamma does not
give the particular unit triangle required here.

The regression field f(t)=floor(t/(pi/6)) mod 4 has tau increment 2 and
2tau increment 0. Its even/odd orbit types each have measure one half.
Since pi/6<gamma<pi/3, a gamma increment is 1 or 2, so equal orbit types
have opposite phases. A pi+gamma increment is 3 or 0, and the palette
union has size three or two. Its side intersections for the given actual
sign vectors and its sixty-degree disjointness also check directly.
Thus it satisfies the named finite/NR/TC relaxations while remaining
four-state globally.

The near-full-measure example is valid as well: odd multiples n of three
can make n*gamma arbitrarily close to pi modulo 2pi, since gamma/pi is
irrational. Remove width-eta bands about the cosine zeros in phase n*t.
The remaining A is tau-invariant, has measure 1-2eta/pi, and a gamma shift
reverses its cosine sign if the phase error is smaller than eta. This
precludes same-phase returns even with measure(A) arbitrarily close to one.
It is a purely angular counterexample; no RealState realization by a
global weak colouring is asserted.
