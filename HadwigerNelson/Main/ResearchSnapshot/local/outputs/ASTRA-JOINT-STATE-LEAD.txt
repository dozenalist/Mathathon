THEOREM AND PROOF
For measurable finite-valued f:R->I and translation d, let
S_i=supp(Lebesgue restricted to {f=i}) and
Q_ij={t:f(t)=i,f(t+d)=j}.
Assume S_i intersects S_j implies Lebesgue(Q_ij)=0 for every ordered pair.
Then a in S_i implies a+d not in S_i, for EVERY a.
Proof: S_i is the finite union over j of supp(Lebesgue restricted to Q_ij).
Choose j whose joint support contains a. Translation continuity and
preservation of null sets put a+d in S_j. If a+d were also in S_i, the
contact hypothesis would make Q_ij null, contradicting its support. QED.
